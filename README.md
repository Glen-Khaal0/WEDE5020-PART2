1NEST POULTRY FARM Website

 About the Project

The 1NEST POULTRY FARM website is a digital platform developed for 1Nest Poultry Farm, founded by **Ms. Selina Khaalo** in the rural village of **Zebediela, Limpopo**.

The farm was established to address the lack of poultry farming opportunities in the community, create employment, and put Zebediela on the map. Inspired by the belief that the egg comes first, 1Nest Poultry Farm aims to grow into a poultry powerhouse where nothing goes to waste.

The farm provides quality poultry products to **households, restaurants, businesses, and organic food enthusiasts**.

## Website Goals

The website is designed to:

* Showcase how our poultry products are produced.
* Introduce customers to 1Nest Poultry Farm.
* Display our products and catalogue.
* Allow customers to make enquiries and place orders.
* Support wholesale egg and poultry distribution.
* Increase brand awareness and customer engagement.
* Provide information for customers who cannot physically visit the farm.
* Provide convenient communication through WhatsApp and online forms.

## Website Features

### Homepage

* Farm imagery
* Company introduction
* Key value propositions
* Clear call-to-action buttons

### About Us

* History of 1Nest Poultry Farm
* Founder information
* Mission and vision
* Information about our farming approach

### Products & Services

* Egg and poultry product catalogue
* Product sizes and pricing
* Wholesale information

### Enquiries & Ordering

* Online enquiry form
* Wholesale order enquiries
* Interactive bulk-order quote calculator

### WhatsApp Integration

Customers can communicate directly with the farm through WhatsApp for enquiries, orders, and general information.

### Location

Interactive Google Maps embeds on the Enquiries and Contact pages help customers locate and verify the farm and central store locations.

## Design & User Experience

The website follows a clean, spacious and visual-focused design that reflects the natural farming environment.

### Colour Palette

* **Deep Earth Green** – represents nature, growth and farming.
* **Warm Yellow** – represents the colour of an egg yolk and provides high-contrast call-to-action elements.
* **White** – represents cleanliness, freshness and the egg itself.

### Typography

* **Headings:** Montserrat
* **Body:** Open Sans / Arial

The website is designed to be:

* Mobile-friendly
* Easy to navigate
* Visually engaging
* Responsive across different screen sizes
* Accessible and user-friendly

Authentic imagery from the farm will be used to build trust and demonstrate how our products are produced.

## Technologies

The website is developed using:

* **HTML** – website structure
* **CSS** – styling and responsive design
* **Google Maps API** – location integration
* **WhatsApp Integration** – customer communication
* **Figma** – wireframes and UI/UX planning
* **Visual Studio Code** – development environment

## Project Structure

```
1Nest-Poultry-Website/
├── index.html          # Homepage
├── about.html          # About Us / Our Story
├── services.html       # Products & pricing
├── enquiries.html      # Online enquiry form + map
├── contact.html        # Contact details + maps
├── styles.css          # Shared stylesheet for all pages
└── _images/             # Farm photos, logo and other imagery
```

All five pages link to a single shared stylesheet (`Mycssstyles.css`)

## Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

* Mobile phones
* Tablets
* Laptops
* Desktop computers

A viewport meta tag is implemented to ensure the website adjusts correctly to different device widths and screen sizes.
